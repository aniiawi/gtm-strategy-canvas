# Strategy Quality Assurance

## Purpose

Prevent a completed-looking Canvas from being mistaken for a coherent, evidence-backed strategy.

## Cross-Canvas Consistency Check

Review the following links and record Pass / Concern / Fail / Not Assessed with rationale:

1. **Market ↔ Customer:** Does the ICP belong to a sufficiently attractive and reachable market?
2. **Customer ↔ Entry:** Does the beachhead reflect urgent customer value and ability to win?
3. **Entry ↔ Product:** Can the product support the selected use case and segment?
4. **Product ↔ Positioning:** Are promised outcomes deliverable and provable?
5. **Customer ↔ Monetization:** Does pricing reflect value, budget, and buying behavior?
6. **Monetization ↔ GTM Motion:** Does the sales and distribution model fit ACV, complexity, and payback?
7. **Product ↔ Launch:** Are product, onboarding, integrations, and support ready for the launch promise?
8. **GTM Motion ↔ Resources:** Are people, budget, and capabilities sufficient for the chosen motion?
9. **Monetization ↔ Economics:** Can the model produce sustainable gross margin, CAC payback, and retention?
10. **Assumptions ↔ Validation:** Are critical assumptions connected to credible tests and decision rules?
11. **Risks ↔ Execution:** Do material risks have owners, mitigations, and triggers?
12. **Metrics ↔ Outcomes:** Do KPIs measure the strategy's intended outcomes and reveal failure early?

## Block-level quality gate

For each of the 12 blocks, verify:
- Objective and scope are clear.
- Relevant questions have been addressed or explicitly marked open.
- Frameworks were selected for utility, not ceremony.
- Decisions and alternatives are visible.
- Evidence status is assigned to material claims.
- Assumptions and unknowns are explicit.
- Dependencies and next actions are recorded.
- No fabricated or unsupported claim is presented as fact.

## Contradiction severity

- **Critical:** Strategy is not viable without resolution.
- **Major:** Material conflict likely to undermine outcomes.
- **Moderate:** Friction that requires mitigation or a conscious trade-off.
- **Minor:** Limited inconsistency with low impact.

Critical contradictions block a Strategically Coherent or Evidence-Backed label unless explicitly resolved or the strategy is reframed.

## Readiness definitions

### Strategic Draft
Some strategic direction exists, but key decisions, evidence, dependencies, or contradictions remain unresolved.

### Strategically Coherent
The major strategic choices fit together and material contradictions are addressed. Evidence may still be incomplete.

### Evidence-Backed
Critical strategic choices are supported by relevant evidence, source limitations are understood, and material contradictions have been addressed.

## Definition of Done

A strategy review is complete only when:
- All 12 blocks have been reviewed, even if some are marked Not Applicable with rationale.
- ICP, beachhead, positioning, monetization, GTM motion, launch, resources, assumptions, risks, and KPIs are explicit.
- Major decisions include rationale, alternatives, evidence status, trade-offs, and next action.
- Critical assumptions have a validation path.
- Risks and dependencies are visible.
- Cross-Canvas contradictions are resolved or clearly disclosed.
- Overall readiness is labelled honestly.

## Final QA report

Provide:
- Overall readiness level.
- Critical and major contradictions.
- Strongest evidence-backed decisions.
- Most consequential unknowns.
- Decisions requiring user approval.
- Next three actions, prioritized by impact and uncertainty.

Never equate completeness, visual polish, or stakeholder consensus with validation.
