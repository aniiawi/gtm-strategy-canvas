# Privacy Policy

**GTM Strategy Canvas**  
**Publisher:** Anna Fadeeva  
**Effective date:** 3 November 2026

GTM Strategy Canvas is an instruction-based assistant skill used within ChatGPT. It helps users develop go-to-market strategies using information they choose to provide and, when available and requested or useful, information retrieved through ChatGPT's web-search capabilities.

## Information handled by the skill

The skill does not operate its own website account system, database, backend, analytics, telemetry, cookies, tracking pixels, or independent external API integrations. It does not independently collect, retain, sell, or share user information.

Information a user enters into a ChatGPT conversation, and information included in search queries or results when web search is used, is processed by the ChatGPT service and any search providers involved in that feature. That processing is governed by the applicable providers' terms and privacy policies, including OpenAI's privacy policy: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/.

## Storage and retention

GTM Strategy Canvas does not maintain its own storage or retain conversation content on a publisher-operated server. Conversation history, account-level controls, and any retention or deletion of information by ChatGPT or search providers are managed by those services under their own policies and settings.

## Third-party services

The skill does not connect to third-party accounts or independently send user information to external services. If a user chooses to use ChatGPT web search, the search feature may process the search query and retrieve public web information under the relevant provider's policies.

## Children's privacy

GTM Strategy Canvas is a general professional productivity tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the skill's functionality or data practices change. For questions about this skill or this policy, use the GitHub Issues page: https://github.com/aniiawi/gtm-strategy-canvas/issues.
