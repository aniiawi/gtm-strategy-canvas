# GTM Strategy Architect — Core Test Cases

Use these cases to test the skill's core behavior across different engagement types. These are scenario prompts and expected behaviors, not scripted ideal answers.

## TC-01 — New B2B SaaS product
**Prompt:** "We are launching an AI assistant for legal teams. Help us build a GTM strategy."
**Expected:** Diagnose stage, geography, target buyer, product maturity, and available evidence. Avoid assuming the market or buyer. Start with the most consequential unknown and build a focused entry hypothesis.

## TC-02 — Existing product entering a new segment
**Prompt:** "Our workflow tool sells to startups. We want to move upmarket to enterprise."
**Expected:** Assess ICP differences, buying committee, security/integration requirements, implementation, sales motion, pricing, and economics. Identify product and operational gaps before recommending a launch.

## TC-03 — GTM strategy review
**Prompt:** "Here is our current GTM plan. Tell me whether it makes sense."
**Expected:** Review supplied material first. Map decisions to Canvas blocks, classify evidence, identify contradictions, and prioritize the highest-impact gaps.

## TC-04 — Specific conversion problem
**Prompt:** "Our website traffic is stable but demo requests are falling."
**Expected:** Bound the funnel and time period; inspect traffic mix, landing-page conversion, message-market fit, form friction, and instrumentation. Ask for the single most diagnostic missing data point.

## TC-05 — Monetization redesign
**Prompt:** "We charge per seat but customers say they get value from automation volume. Should we switch to usage pricing?"
**Expected:** Compare value metrics, predictability, buyer preferences, margin exposure, expansion dynamics, and migration risks. Recommend research or controlled tests before asserting a final model.

## TC-06 — Positioning challenge
**Prompt:** "We want to position as the leading AI platform for every business."
**Expected:** Challenge generic breadth; identify alternatives, target segment, differentiated outcome, proof, and category implications. Do not polish an ungrounded claim.

## TC-07 — Channel allocation
**Prompt:** "We have a small budget. Should we invest in content, outbound, or paid search?"
**Expected:** Assess audience intent, ACV, sales cycle, current assets, time-to-learning, and channel economics. Recommend a constrained test or conditional priority.

## TC-08 — Launch readiness
**Prompt:** "Marketing wants to announce next month, but onboarding is still manual."
**Expected:** Assess customer impact, support capacity, product readiness, launch tier, mitigations, and go/no-go criteria. Make the trade-off explicit.

## TC-09 — Expansion economics
**Prompt:** "We want to double revenue next year with the same team and budget."
**Expected:** Model the required funnel and capacity implications. Surface assumptions and identify levers or contradictions rather than repeating the target.

## TC-10 — Research request
**Prompt:** "Research the competitors and tell me where we can win."
**Expected:** Define the relevant market and competitor set; use dated credible sources; distinguish observed facts from inference; connect findings to a specific strategic decision.

## TC-11 — User rejects a recommended path
**Prompt:** "I understand the risks, but we are committed to a partner-led motion."
**Expected:** Treat the decision as a constraint unless the user invites challenge. Explore partner profile, incentives, enablement, economics, ownership, and validation.

## TC-12 — Missing information
**Prompt:** "Just tell me which segment to target. I don't have data."
**Expected:** State what cannot be concluded, offer a provisional decision framework, and ask one high-value question or propose a fast discovery test.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Agent identifies the scenario and adapts its approach.
- [ ] Agent does not force all 12 blocks into every interaction.
- [ ] Agent preserves the canonical block structure when presenting the full Canvas.
- [ ] Agent asks only consequential questions.
- [ ] Agent labels evidence and uncertainty.
- [ ] Agent records decisions and trade-offs.
- [ ] Agent detects cross-block contradictions.
- [ ] Agent ends with concrete next actions.
- [ ] Agent does not claim a file or research artifact exists unless verified.
