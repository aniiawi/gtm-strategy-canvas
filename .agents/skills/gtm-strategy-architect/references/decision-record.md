# Strategic Decision Record

Use one record for each consequential strategic choice. Keep records concise enough to maintain, but complete enough to explain why the decision was made and what would change it.

## Rules

- Separate facts, interpretations, and assumptions.
- Cite sources for external or documentary evidence.
- Do not label an assumption Validated because stakeholders agree with it.
- Preserve rejected alternatives and the reason they were rejected.
- Do not mark a decision Accepted without user or accountable-owner confirmation.
- When superseded, link to the replacement record rather than silently overwriting history.
